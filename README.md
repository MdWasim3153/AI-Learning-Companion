# AI Learning Companion

> Making Artificial Intelligence understandable for everyone, one conversation at a time.

AI Learning Companion helps everyday people understand, trust, and confidently use Artificial Intelligence through simple 15-minute daily learning experiences.

15 minutes a day. One lesson. One conversation. A lifetime skill.

## Why this project exists
Most people I know are curious about AI but never actually learn it. Not because they're not smart enough, but because everything out there is either a 40-hour course they'll never finish or a YouTube video that assumes you already know half the terms.

This project was created to bridge that gap — something you could do on a coffee break, every day, that actually adds up to real understanding instead of just more bookmarked tabs.

That's what this is. No prerequisites, no jargon dump on day one. Just one idea a day, explained like a person would explain it to a friend.

## What it actually does
- **One lesson a day, 15 minutes, done.** Not a syllabus you fall behind on.
- **Conversational, not a lecture.** You can ask "Wait, what does that mean?" and get a real answer.
- **Focused on understanding, not tricks.** The goal is understanding AI instead of memorizing prompt hacks.
- **Small daily wins that stack up.** Learn consistently and build lasting confidence.
