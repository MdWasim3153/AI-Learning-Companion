# Persona 1 — The Future Builder

Name: Aarav Sharma (example persona)
Age: 20
Occupation: College Student
Education: Bachelor's Degree (Engineering/Commerce/Arts)
Technical Knowledge: Comfortable with smartphones and computers but has never used AI seriously.

## Background
Aarav frequently hears about ChatGPT, AI tools, and automation from friends, YouTube, LinkedIn, and social media. He believes AI is becoming an essential skill for future careers but doesn't know where to start.

## Goals
Learn AI from scratch.
Improve assignments and projects.
Prepare for internships and placements.
Build confidence in using AI tools.
Gain a competitive advantage in the job market.

## Pain Points
Doesn't know which AI tools to use.
Overwhelmed by technical content.
Afraid of learning "the wrong way."
Watches many tutorials but struggles to apply them.

## Motivation
"If AI is the future, I want to learn it before I enter the job market."

## AI Use Cases
Assignment assistance
Research
Resume improvement
Interview preparation
Presentation creation
Learning new subjects

# Persona 2 — The Professional Accelerator

Name: Priya Mehta (example persona)
Age: 42
Occupation: Senior Project Manager / Business Owner
Technical Knowledge: Uses Microsoft Office, email, and productivity tools daily but has limited AI experience.

## Background
Priya has spent years building her career. Recently, she has noticed colleagues using AI to write emails, create reports, analyze data, and automate repetitive work. She worries about falling behind if she doesn't learn these tools.

## Goals
Increase productivity.
Save time on repetitive tasks.
Stay relevant in a changing workplace.
Use AI to make better business decisions.
Learn practical AI without studying technical theory.

## Pain Points
Limited time to learn.
Unsure which AI tools are trustworthy.
Feels intimidated by technical terminology.
Doesn't know how to write effective prompts.

## Motivation
"I don't want AI to replace me—I want AI to help me become better at my work."

## AI Use Cases
Email drafting
Meeting summaries
Document creation
Data analysis
Business planning
Marketing content
Customer communication

# Persona 3 — The Curious Lifelong Learner

Name: Professor Anil Verma (example persona)

Age: 72
Occupation: Retired Professor
Technical Knowledge: Comfortable using a smartphone, email, and web browsing. Very little exposure to AI.

## Background
Professor Verma has dedicated his life to teaching and learning. He now hears about Artificial Intelligence everywhere and wants to understand what it is, what it can do, and how it can help in everyday life. He is not trying to build a career in AI—he simply wants to continue learning and remain connected to the modern world.

## Goals
Understand AI without technical jargon.
Explore practical everyday uses.
Continue lifelong learning.
Use AI to simplify daily tasks.
Stay informed about modern technology.


## Pain Points
Finds most AI resources too technical.
Feels hesitant to ask basic questions.
Unsure how to interact with AI effectively.
Wants explanations in simple, patient language.


## Motivation
"Learning has no age limit. I simply want to understand this technology that everyone is talking about."

## AI Use Cases
Asking questions
Reading and summarizing articles
Planning travel
Writing letters and emails
Health and wellness information (general guidance)
Learning new hobbies
Organizing daily activities
