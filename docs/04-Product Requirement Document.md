# 04 - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Purpose
This document defines the product requirements for the **AI Learning Companion**. It serves as the primary reference for product planning, design, development, testing, and future enhancements.

The PRD answers one fundamental question:

> **What exactly are we building, for whom, and why?**

---

# Background
Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming a part of everyday life, yet many people avoid using it because they believe it is difficult, technical, or only meant for programmers.

The AI Learning Companion aims to remove this fear by providing a structured, practical, and confidence-building learning experience. Instead of teaching complex AI theory, the platform focuses on helping learners understand how to communicate effectively with AI using real-world examples, structured prompting techniques such as COSTAR, and hands-on practice.

The ultimate goal is to make AI feel like a trusted everyday companion rather than an intimidating technology.

---

# Product Vision
Create the world's most trusted AI learning platform that enables anyone—regardless of age or technical background—to confidently use Artificial Intelligence in their personal and professional lives.

---

# Product Mission
Make AI simple, approachable, practical, and enjoyable by providing structured daily learning, personalized guidance, and real-world practice that builds confidence rather than fear.

---

# Product Goals
The AI Learning Companion should:

* Remove the fear associated with Artificial Intelligence.
* Teach users how to communicate effectively with AI.
* Build confidence through small daily learning sessions.
* Encourage practical learning rather than theoretical knowledge.
* Help users integrate AI into their everyday personal and professional activities.
* Make AI education accessible to learners of all ages and technical backgrounds.

---

# Target Audience
The initial version of the product is designed for:

* Curious beginners
* Students
* Teachers
* Working professionals
* Entrepreneurs
* Senior citizens

No prior AI knowledge is required.

---

# Problem Statement
Many people believe they must learn Artificial Intelligence before they can use it.

In reality, they only need to learn how to communicate with AI effectively.

This product bridges that gap.

---

# Version 1 Scope (MVP)
The first version of the application will include:

### User Management
* User Registration
* Login
* Profile Management
* Password Reset

---

### Learning Experience
* Structured 30-day learning program
* Daily lessons (15–20 minutes)
* Interactive AI explanations
* Practical exercises
* Real-world examples
* Daily quizzes
* Progress tracking

---

### AI Features
* AI-powered learning companion
* Prompt-writing guidance
* COSTAR framework examples
* Personalized AI assistance during lessons
* Question and answer support

---

### Gamification
* Daily streaks
* Learning milestones
* Achievement badges
* Course completion certificate

---

### Progress Tracking
* Daily progress
* Quiz scores
* Completion percentage
* Learning history

---

### User Dashboard
* Current lesson
* Learning progress
* Recommended next lesson
* Daily goals
* Recent achievements

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)
The following features are intentionally excluded from the MVP:

* Voice conversations
* AI avatar mentor
* Community discussions
* Teacher dashboard
* Parent dashboard
* Enterprise edition
* Offline mode
* Multi-language support
* AI interview preparation
* Resume builder
* Advanced analytics

These ideas will be evaluated after the MVP is successfully released.

---

# Functional Requirements
The system shall:

* Allow users to create an account.
* Authenticate users securely.
* Deliver lessons sequentially.
* Prevent skipping mandatory lessons and quizzes.
* Record learning progress.
* Store quiz results.
* Recommend the next lesson automatically.
* Allow users to revisit completed lessons.
* Provide AI assistance during learning.
* Generate a completion certificate after finishing the course.

---

# Non-Functional Requirements
The application should:

* Be easy for first-time users.
* Support Android devices initially.
* Maintain secure authentication and user data.
* Deliver lessons with minimal loading time.
* Be scalable for future feature additions.
* Provide a responsive and intuitive user experience.
* Be reliable and available for daily learning.

---

# User Stories

### As a beginner
I want to understand AI without technical jargon so that I can confidently start using it.

---

### As a student
I want practical AI examples so I can improve my learning and assignments.

---

### As a working professional
I want to use AI to save time and increase productivity.

---

### As a teacher
I want AI to help create better educational content.

---

### As a senior citizen
I want simple explanations so I can use AI comfortably without feeling overwhelmed.

---

# Success Metrics
The MVP will be considered successful if:

* Users complete the 30-day learning journey.
* Daily engagement remains consistently high.
* Users report increased confidence in using AI.
* Lesson completion rates exceed predefined targets.
* Quiz performance improves over time.
* Users begin using AI in their daily activities.

---

# Assumptions
* Most users will have little or no AI experience.
* Users prefer short daily learning sessions over lengthy courses.
* Practical examples are more effective than theoretical lectures.
* Confidence grows through consistent practice and feedback.

---

# Constraints
* Version 1 targets Android devices.
* English will be the initial language.
* Internet connectivity is required.
* Development will prioritize simplicity over feature richness.

---

# Risks
* Users may abandon learning if lessons become too technical.
* AI responses may occasionally require validation or refinement.
* Rapid advancements in AI may require frequent curriculum updates.
* Keeping learners engaged for the full 30-day journey may be challenging.

---

# Acceptance Criteria
The MVP will be considered complete when:

* Users can register and log in.
* The complete 30-day curriculum is available.
* Lessons, quizzes, and progress tracking function correctly.
* AI guidance is integrated into lessons.
* Users receive certificates upon completion.
* The application provides a smooth and intuitive learning experience.
